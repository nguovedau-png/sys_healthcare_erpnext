# Browser-verified research findings — 2026-08-28

## BMJ Innovations / Docosan case
Source: https://innovations.bmj.com/content/7/Suppl_1/s16

The article reports that Vietnamese patients are more likely to be satisfied when given a simple digital platform to find, book, and compare healthcare options. It also describes provider value from a marketplace that expands discovery beyond word-of-mouth and notes widespread smartphone use as an enabler. Product implication: prioritize mobile-first discovery, transparent comparison, simple booking, and provider acquisition/portal workflows rather than starting as a broad super-app.

## Ministry of Industry and Trade — Vietnam e-commerce 2024
Source: https://moit.gov.vn/khoa-hoc-va-cong-nghe/thuong-mai-dien-tu-viet-nam-nam-2024-nhung-buoc-tien-va-thach-thuc.html

The page states that cashless payments, digital wallets, and digital banking have grown, while COD remains common outside major urban areas because of lower trust in electronic payments. It also identifies logistics gaps outside cities and continuing concerns about quality, after-sales service, and personal-data security. Product implication: support QR/bank/wallet payment adapters plus pay-at-facility/COD-like fallback where relevant, provide clear refunds and receipts, and design trust/safety and regional operations explicitly.

## DLA Piper — Vietnam data protection update
Source: https://www.dlapiperdataprotection.com/?t=law&c=VN

The source states that Law 91/2025/QH15 on Personal Data Protection took effect on 1 January 2026, with Decree 356/2025/ND-CP taking effect alongside it and replacing Decree 13/2023/ND-CP as the guiding decree. It emphasizes purpose limitation, accuracy/correction, proportional retention, organizational/technical/human safeguards, and prevention/detection/handling of violations. Product implication: update compliance documents and data-governance backlog for the 2026 law, not only the older decree, with purpose-specific consent/legal basis, correction, retention, access control, incident handling, and audit evidence.

## Medpro — direct competitor
Source: https://medpro.vn/

The official site presents a multi-channel service with web/app, hotline, facility and medical-service discovery, online booking, remote consultation/video, examination booking outside normal hours, corporate health checks, and patient-facing flows. Its public positioning emphasizes connecting users with hospitals, clinics, and services, and shows social channels including Zalo and Facebook. Product implication: a new platform must not compete only on generic booking; its defensible wedge should be clinic operations after booking, reliable queue/encounter/billing synchronization, and transparent trust signals, while still supporting Vietnamese multi-channel discovery and family workflows.
